# Wallora 4K Wallpaper Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

Wallora 4K Wallpaper is a wallpaper browser (`com.wallora.wallpaper`). We do not operate a user account system or collect personal data on our servers.

## On your device

- **Favorites:** A list of wallpaper IDs you liked, stored locally.
- **Photos:** When you tap Like or Download, the app saves a copy to your photo library after you grant permission.

## Permissions

- **Photo library (add):** Only when you choose to save a wallpaper.

## Third parties

No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs are included.

## Contact

Use the support email you provide in App Store Connect.
